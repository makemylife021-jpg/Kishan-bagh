# Jaipur leads: businesses without a website

`jaipur-leads.csv` lists Jaipur businesses whose public listings (Google, Justdial) showed no website of their own on 2026-11-02.

Priority 1 means a high rating with many reviews: an established customer base and still no website.

**Before you call:** search the business name on Google once. A listing may be missing a website that does exist.

## WhatsApp pitch (Hinglish)

> Namaste ji, main [Naam] Jaipur se. Google par aapki [business] ki rating bahut acchi hai ([rating] ⭐, [reviews] reviews), lekin aapki apni website nahi dikhi.
> Main chhote businesses ke liye website banata hoon, jisme WhatsApp button, Google Maps aur enquiry form hota hai.
> Aapke liye ek free demo bana kar 2 din mein dikha sakta hoon. Pasand aaye tabhi aage baat karenge. Kya main demo bana doon?
