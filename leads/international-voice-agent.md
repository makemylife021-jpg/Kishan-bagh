## What agencies charge (published 2026 prices)

- Starter AI receptionist: $900-$2,500 setup + $290-$700/month (BX, Growth100x, Ciela guide)
- Booking agent with calendar + CRM: $2,500-$4,000 setup + $450-$650/month (Ciela guide)
- UK: GBP 300-1,500/month + GBP 500-2,500 setup (Eldris guide)
- Running cost: about $0.07-$0.31 per call minute on Retell (~$0.10 typical) + ~$2/month per number, paid by the client

## Best niches
HVAC / plumbing / electricians, dental clinics, estate agents, restaurants, med spas: businesses that lose money on every missed call.

## Rules for direct outreach
- US: no cold robocalls or cold SMS without consent (TCPA). Cold email is allowed with your real address and an unsubscribe line (CAN-SPAM).
- UK/EU: cold email to companies is OK under PECR if you give an opt-out. Do not cold-call numbers on the TPS register.
- The agent must tell callers it is an AI, and say so if calls are recorded.
