## What it does
- Answers as "Ava, the virtual assistant" and says it is an AI if asked
- Collects name, phone, address, issue and preferred arrival window, then reads them back
- Handles emergencies safely: gas smell or CO means leave the house and call 911; no heat or AC goes to urgent callback
- Quotes only the $89 diagnostic fee and never takes card details

## 5 test calls before you show a client
1. Normal booking: "My AC is blowing warm air."
2. Emergency: "I smell gas in the kitchen."
3. Price question: "How much for a new water heater?" (it should not invent a price)
4. Asking for a person: "Can I talk to the owner?"
5. Angry customer: "Your tech never showed up yesterday!"

Record calls 1 and 2 on your phone. Those two clips are your portfolio.
